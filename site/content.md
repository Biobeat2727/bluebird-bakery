## Jill

- Jill Severson founded Bluebird Bakery in October 2019.
- Guiding principles: quality ingredients, fresh product, supporting the local and regional community.
- Before the storefront she baked bread at home for the Sandpoint Farmers' Market.
- Before Sandpoint: pastry chef at Majestic Valley Wilderness Lodge and Heli Ski, Glacier View, Alaska.
- Before that: over a decade in Seattle at Mistral Kitchen, The Essential Baking Company, The Fairmont Olympic Hotel.
- Born in Thousand Oaks, California. Pastry Arts at The French Culinary Institute and Bread at the International Culinary Center, New York. Worked at Jacques Torres Chocolate while in school.
- Moved to Sandpoint for nature, a smaller community and a more balanced life. Snowboards, boats, camps.
