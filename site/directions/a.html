Our story

Started at the Sandpoint Farmers' Market

Before the storefront, Jill baked bread at home and sold it at the Sandpoint Farmers' Market. She trained in pastry and bread in New York, spent over a decade in Seattle kitchens and bakeries, then worked as a pastry chef at a heli-ski lodge in Alaska.

She moved to Sandpoint for a smaller town and more time outside. When she's not at the bakery she's snowboarding or camping.
